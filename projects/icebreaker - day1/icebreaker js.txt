Here's a **team-based problem-solving game** concept called **"Escape the Lab!"**. It's a browser-based collaborative **logic puzzle game** built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The idea is to **solve riddles and logic puzzles as a team** to escape a locked virtual lab.

---

### 🔐 Game Concept: Escape the Lab!

You and your team are trapped in a lab with a digital lock. To escape, you must solve 3 logic puzzles—each one unlocks part of the door code. When all 3 parts are solved, the door opens.

---

### 🎯 Features

* **Collaborative**: Best played as a team (on one device or sharing answers remotely).
* **3 Levels**: Each puzzle gives part of a final 3-digit escape code.
* **Timer**: Adds tension to the game.
* **Hints**: Optional hints available after some time.
* **Minimal UI**: Clean and focused layout.

---

### 💡 Puzzle Examples

1. **Logic Puzzle**: "Three boxes, one contains the key. One label is true. Choose wisely."
2. **Riddle**: “I speak without a mouth and hear without ears. What am I?”
3. **Pattern Challenge**: Number sequences or symbol decoding.
